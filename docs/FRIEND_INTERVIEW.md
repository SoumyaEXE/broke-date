# 10 minutes with Subarna (do this, don't write his lines for him)

The theme is *build for a friend*, so the strongest paragraph in the post is his, in his words. Sit with him, his
laptop, Wi-Fi off. Let him drive. Write down what he says verbatim, then ask before quoting any of it.

## Before he touches it (2 min)
1. "What happens around the 20th of the month?" (the problem, in his words)
2. "How do you decide today if you can afford something?" (what he does now)

## While he uses it (5 min) — don't explain anything unless he's stuck
3. Ask him to type his own question in Ask. Note the exact question he types.
4. Let him switch one plan on or off. "What did that number tell you?"
5. Open Time machine on a month he remembers. "Is that how that month felt?"

## After (3 min)
6. "Would you trust the safe-to-spend number? Why / why not?"
7. "What's wrong with it? What would you change?" (put his criticism in the post; judges trust it)
8. "Would you use this next month?" (yes/no + why)

## Rules for the post
- Quote him exactly; fix nothing but typos. If he says it's confusing, say that.
- Numbers about his money: only from `brokedate share-card -s <his subject>` (relative: months, days of notice).
  No rupee amounts, no dates, no merchants, no screenshots of his real data.
- He reads the draft and says OK before it's published.
